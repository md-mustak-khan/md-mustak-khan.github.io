Educational Activities

In Silico Innovation: Computer-Aided Drug Design and Network Pharmacology

Training Role: Instructor

Platform: BioPC – A Bioinformatics Lab of Research and Training

Download Course Syllabus

In Silico Innovation: Computer-Aided Drug Design and Network Pharmacology is a specialized course focused on computational approaches to modern drug discovery. The course integrates bioinformatics, computer-aided drug design (CADD), and network pharmacology to provide rigorous theoretical and practical training in systems-level drug discovery.

Course Outcomes

  • Understand core concepts of computer-aided drug design and network pharmacology
  • Apply bioinformatics tools for target analysis and molecular interpretation
  • Develop a stronger foundation in translational bioinformatics and interdisciplinary research
