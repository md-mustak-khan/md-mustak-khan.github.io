About My Academy and Research Journey

Biochemistry, molecular biology, and interdisciplinary scientific discovery

Personal Statement

I am an early-career researcher with a strong foundation in biochemistry, molecular biology, and bioinformatics, driven by the goal of translating scientific knowledge into meaningful discoveries in health, nutrition, and therapeutic development. My work bridges wet-lab experimentation, computational analysis, and scientific communication, with a particular focus on plant-derived compounds, model organism research, and interdisciplinary innovation.
