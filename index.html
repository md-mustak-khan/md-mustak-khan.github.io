About

Discover My Journey

Brief summary of background, education, and research interests.

Md. Mustak Khan — Researcher in Phytomedicine, Molecular Biology & Bioinformatics. BSc. & MS (University of Chittagong).

I work across the Laboratory of Applied Biosciences and the Laboratory of Food Science & Nutraceutical as a Research Assistant and Lab Manager, where I oversee day-to-day lab operations, mentor students, and design experiments using model organisms. My MS research combined GC–MS phytochemical profiling with molecular docking, ADMET prediction, and in vivo validation in C. elegans to study the antioxidant properties of Houttuynia cordata. I lead computational and translational projects at BioPC—Bioinformatics Lab of Research and Training, develop web tools for academic events, and provide training in bioinformatics and experimental methods. I regularly use tools such as AutoDock Vina, SwissADME, and Python/R for analysis and visualization.

Earlier, I trained as a research intern at CHIRAL Bangladesh and contributed to the aiHealth Lab, gaining hands-on exposure to AI and ML applications in biomedical research. My work sits at the intersection of wet-lab biology and computational science, with interests in natural products, model-organism studies, and bioinformatics-driven discovery. I contribute to collaborative publications and mentor students through workshops and internships.
